THE OPPORTUNITY

W7 opened in October 2025 with three courts on the Glebe. Your brand goes where those people look: on the court, on the league site, and in every result email.

THE LEAGUES

250+ players with a game in the diary every week.

AUTUMN/WINTER BOX LEAGUE
20 boxes · 100 teams · 200 players. Five teams a box, everyone plays everyone, promotion and relegation each cycle. Players enter their own scores online and confirm their opponents' with a tap: 80 results entered by players in the first three weeks, and 87 of 100 teams have used the site.
SUMMER LEAGUE 2026
60 teams · 5 divisions · 330 fixtures. A round-robin season from June, then 24 teams into two tiered knockouts and a finals day at the club on 27 September, the club's first birthday.
EVERY RESULT, AN EMAIL
When a score goes in, both teams get an email with the result and the box table. A title sponsor's name is on every one of them.
A STAND-IN LIST
Players outside the league put their name down to fill in when a team is short. More people on the courts, more people on the site.
A FINALS DAY
Both tiers play their finals on one Sunday at the club, with the whole membership invited. The natural home for a tournament sponsor.
